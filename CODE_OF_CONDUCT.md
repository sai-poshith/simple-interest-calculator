# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation respectful and inclusive.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful
- Avoid harassment

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Maintainers are responsible for enforcing rules.

## Scope
Applies to all contributors.

## Enforcement
Violations will be reviewed and handled.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans.
